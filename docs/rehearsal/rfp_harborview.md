# Request for Proposal — Community Library Booking Portal

**Client:** Harborview District Libraries (fictional)
**Sector:** Public libraries, 4 branches

## Background
Harborview runs four branch libraries. Room bookings, event sign-ups and equipment loans are
handled by phone and paper, and the branches cannot see each other's calendars. We want one
public portal that residents use from their phones and that staff manage from one place.

## Requirements
1. A **public booking portal** for meeting rooms and events across all 4 branches, usable on a phone.
2. **Automatic reminders** by email 24 hours before a booking, with a one-click cancel link.
3. Integration with our **existing Koha library system** for patron identity — patrons log in with their library card; **no replacement of Koha**.
4. A **staff calendar** showing every branch, with the ability to block rooms for maintenance.
5. **Accessibility**: the public portal must meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
6. **Training and handover**: two on-site sessions per branch and a written admin guide.
7. A clear statement of **assumptions, dependencies and risks**.

## Budget
€45,000–€60,000 in total, including the first year of hosting and support.

## Timeline
A working portal for one branch within 2 months; all four branches within 4 months.
