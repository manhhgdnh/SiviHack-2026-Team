# Proposal: Harborview Bookings Platform

**Submitted by:** Lanternworks Digital (fictional)

## Our Understanding
Harborview's four branches need a single place for residents to book rooms and events and for
staff to see every branch's calendar. Today this runs on phone calls and paper, so double
bookings and missed reminders are common.

## Proposed Solution
- **Resident portal**: a responsive web app for room and event bookings across the four branches, built for phones first.
- **Reminders**: email reminders 24 hours ahead with a cancel link, plus optional SMS.
- **Identity**: we will migrate patron records out of Koha into our own accounts service so that residents get a modern single sign-on across the portal and future services.
- **Staff calendar**: one calendar for all branches, with maintenance blocks and a waiting list.
- **Accessibility**: we follow accessibility best practices throughout.

## Timeline
We will start with discovery and aim to have the first branch live promptly, followed by the
remaining branches in due course. Exact dates will be confirmed once discovery is complete.
